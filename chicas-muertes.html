chicas muertes book design

  • Chicas Muertes is a non-fiction essay by Argentinian author Selva Almada that grapples with the issue of femicide in Argentina and South and Central America at large. My design approach was to emphasize the gravity and seriousness of the topic while paying respect to the victims of femicide, using tally marks to highlight the magnitude and increasing banality of these cases, and the purple color as a reference to the Ni Una Menos movement started in Argentina to combat gender based violence.
